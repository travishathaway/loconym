## Logo

The logator logo is a derivative mashup of two open-source emoji artworks:

- **Magnifying glass**: [Twemoji](https://github.com/twitter/twemoji), Copyright Twitter, Inc and other
  contributors. Graphics licensed under [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Crocodile**: [Noto Emoji](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji), Copyright Google Inc., licensed under the
  [Apache License 2.0](assets/logo/NOTO-LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt).

The full-detail logos reuse the original artwork, modified and combined: the magnifying glass has its handle tucked
behind the crocodile. The small version used as the favicon is a simplified redrawing inspired by those shapes and
colors.
